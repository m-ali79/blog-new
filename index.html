03 · questions

the things people actually ask.

will google punish ai-written content?

google’s stated position punishes content made for rankings instead of people — not the tool that typed it. nib's drafts go through the same scorecard a human editor would apply, and the approve step keeps a person accountable for every post.

how does it match my brand voice?

stage 1.0 builds a voice profile from your existing posts — sentence length, structure, vocabulary. every draft is written against that profile, so the output reads like your blog, not a press release.

can it publish without me?

no. nib files everything as a wordpress draft and stops. the publish button is yours — that’s the whole point of the design.

what if the draft isn’t good?

each section can be regenerated with a nudge — "shorter", "more examples", "less jargon". the 0–100 score tells you exactly what's weak before you spend time reading.

does it work outside wordpress?

wordpress first — it's where most business blogs live. webflow and shopify are on the roadmap. if you’re on another stack, the drafts export as clean markdown.
